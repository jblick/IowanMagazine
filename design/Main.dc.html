The Iowan: a new home on the web

Proposed structure and design direction for a magazine site that sells subscriptions and back issues, sells ads, takes submissions and promotes Heuss Printing. Nothing here is final. Your answers to the interview questions decide what gets built.

The base site today

Crawled from heuss.presencehost.net/customerdesigns/the-iowan-magazine.html (the portal address you gave returns a 404; the Iowan page sits under Customer Designs). It is a single catalog page on Heuss Printing's PrinterPresence site: a short intro, then six products, then Heuss's generic printing footer. I read the page markup, not a rendered screenshot, so visual critiques are still to come.

What it sells

  • 1 year, $24 (or as a gift)
  • 2 years, $38 (or as a gift)
  • 1 year + "I am Iowan" tee, $53
  • "I am Iowan" tee alone, $30 (XS to 6XL)

What gets in the way

  • Gift is a separate product, not a choice on one
  • Item numbers run 01, 03, 02, 04 on the detail pages
  • "Mailing internationally?" has no price or note
  • Subtotal reads N/A until a choice is made
  • Six products in one flat list, no best-value cue

What is missing

  • Any magazine content, covers or photography
  • Issues per year, delivery timing, renewal terms
  • Back issues, advertising, submissions, events
  • A way for existing readers to manage a subscription
  • Magazine branding: the footer and nav are Heuss's

A first pass at fixing the buying page is on the next artboard, using only the real products and prices above. Anything the page does not state is a bracketed placeholder.

Homepage wireframe, top to bottom

  1. Masthead and utility bar: logo, primary nav, Subscribe button always visible, account link, search.
  2. Cover story hero: one large Iowa photograph, headline, deck, and the current cover with a Subscribe call to action beside it.
  3. Latest stories: a magazine-style grid with one lead story and four smaller ones.
  4. Subscribe band: price, term and a gift option, with a visible "manage my subscription" link for existing readers.
  5. Events near you: the next four calendar items.
  6. Back issues: a row of covers with prices and quick add.
  7. Our advertisers: a respectful sponsor strip with a link to the full directory.
  8. Newsletter and submissions: two side-by-side prompts.
  9. Footer: "Printed by Heuss Printing" with a quote link, contact details and social links.

Ideas beyond your list

Digital edition and archive search. Flip-through of each issue, with paywalled full-text search.
Gift subscriptions. A seasonal landing page with a card sent by email.
Newsletter. A weekly email that feeds the site and gives advertisers another slot.
Advertiser profile pages. Sold as a package: print ad plus listing, with tracked clicks.
Map of Iowa. Stories and events pinned by county or town.
Photo contest. Annual reader submissions that become content and a print feature.
Group and school rates. Bulk subscriptions for libraries and classrooms.
Accessibility and speed. WCAG AA, mobile-first, fast pages for rural connections.

Design direction

Taken from the magazine's own covers: a tall, high-contrast serif in capitals for the masthead (set here in a close Google Fonts match, since the site has no standalone logo file), deep teal as the main color with a warm gold accent, cream pages, and large cover and landscape photography. Body text in a clean sans. Heuss Printing appears as a "Printed by" credit with its real logo (orange and blue) in the footer. Subscribe and account actions stay one click away on every page. If The Iowan has a master logo file or brand guide, I will swap it in.
