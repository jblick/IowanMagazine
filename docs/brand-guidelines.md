## 1. Brand Foundation

### Who we are

The Iowan is Iowa's original state magazine, published since 1952. Each issue travels to a different corner of
the state for its people, places, food, history, events and landscape, told through long-form stories and large,
generous photography. It is printed in Ames by Heuss Printing.

### Brand promise

> **The people. The places. The stories. The life.**

This four-beat line (from the magazine's Instagram bio) is the master tagline. Use it whole, in this order,
with the periods. It can be set in the body sans in small caps or as an Oswald kicker.

### Positioning statement

For Iowans, former Iowans and people who simply love the state, The Iowan is the magazine that finds the
remarkable in the ordinary corners of Iowa, because it has been looking closely, one county at a time, since 1952.

### Supporting description (from the current site)

> The Iowan is your gateway to travel destinations, history and adventure, food and events.

Use for meta descriptions, catalog listings and directory blurbs.

### Brand pillars

| Pillar | What it means | Cover evidence |
|--------|---------------|----------------|
| **People** | Real Iowans, named, in their own places | "Iowa balloon pilot lives his dream", "Simple living: Kaleb Wyse bought his grandparents' Iowa farm" |
| **Places** | Towns, farms, bookstores, skylines, rivers | "A bookworm's guide to Iowa bookstores", "Views of Iowa from above" |
| **Stories** | History, heritage, culture, honest personal accounts | "Celebrating Danish heritage in western Iowa", "One Iowan's alcoholism story" |
| **The life** | Food, outdoors, arts, things to do this season | "Go to Marshalltown for Iowa's best burger", "The Storm Lake chainsaw masterpieces" |

### Audiences

The core readership skews older, academic and politically liberal; many subscriptions are held by schools and libraries.

| Audience | Need | What we offer them |
|----------|------|-------------------|
| Longtime subscribers | Their next issue, renewals, address changes | Reliable delivery, an easy "Manage my subscription" path |
| Schools and libraries (key institutional subscribers) | Purchase orders, invoicing, multiple copies, a complete print run | Dedicated institutional page, quote form, catalog details |
| Academic and older readers | Long-form reading, research, comfortable type | Large type and text-size control, citation tool, story search |
| Gift givers | A meaningful present for an Iowan (often an expat or parent) | Gift subscription, tee bundle |
| Day-trippers and travelers | Where to go this weekend | Destination stories, events calendar |
| Iowa expats | A connection to home | Nostalgia, heritage, "I am Iowan" merchandise |
| Iowa businesses | Reaching engaged, loyal in-state readers | Print ads, newsletter sponsorship, advertiser profiles |
| Writers and photographers | A respected place to publish Iowa work | Clear guidelines and submission forms |

## 2. Masthead and Logo

### The masthead

The brand mark is the wordmark **THE IOWAN**, set in all capitals in a high-contrast, bracketed serif,
spaced wide across the full trim width of the cover. No standalone vector logo was available for this
document; on the web it is typeset in **Libre Caslon Text 400, letter-spacing 0.04em**, which is the
closest free match. **[TO CONFIRM: obtain the master masthead file (vector) and the name of the print typeface
from the art director.]**

### Variants

| Variant | Use | Notes |
|---------|-----|-------|
| Cover masthead | Print covers | Full-bleed width, top of cover, color changes per issue (see 3.4) |
| Web masthead | Site header, emails | Libre Caslon Text, 28 to 40px, Iowan Teal by default |
| Reversed | Dark photos, footer | Cream #F7F3EA or Harvest Gold #F2C28C |
| "i am IOWAN" lockup | Merchandise only | Lowercase italic "i am" + IOWAN in the masthead serif, white on heather navy |
| Social avatar | Instagram, Facebook | "THE IOWAN" stacked or "TI" monogram on Iowan Teal **[TO CONFIRM existing avatar]** |

### Clear space and minimum size

- Clear space on all sides equals the height of the letter "I" in the masthead.
- Minimum web size: 120px wide. Minimum print size: 30mm wide.
- On covers, cover lines and photo subjects may overlap the bottom of the masthead (as on the balloon issue), but
  never cover more than a third of the height of any letter, and never the word THE.

### Don'ts

- Don't set the masthead in lowercase or title case.
- Don't stretch, condense, outline, add drop shadows or bevels to it.
- Don't put the masthead in a box or banner; it sits directly on the photograph or page.
- Don't use more than one color in the masthead.
- Don't drop "THE".
- Don't use the Heuss Printing logo in a way that reads as the magazine's own logo.

## 5. Voice and Tone

### Brand personality

The Iowan sounds like a well-traveled neighbor who has been everywhere in the state and still gets excited
about a good small-town bookstore. It is proud of Iowa without being boastful, and curious without being
condescending to rural life.

| Trait | We are | We are not |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Warm** | Friendly, generous, neighborly | Gushing, saccharine |
| **Curious** | Asking why, going to see for ourselves | Know-it-all, listicle-shallow |
| **Proud** | Confident that Iowa is worth the trip | Defensive, "flyover" jokes, boosterish |
| **Plainspoken** | Clear, concrete, Midwestern-direct | Folksy caricature, corporate jargon |
| **Respectful** | Honest stories told with care ("One Iowan's alcoholism story") | Sensational, clickbait |

### Writing principles

1. **Name the place.** Every story is anchored to a town, county or region: "Go to Marshalltown for Iowa's best burger," not "This burger joint is worth the drive."
2. **Lead with people.** Use real names and roles. "Kaleb Wyse bought his grandparents' Iowa farm."
3. **Verb-first, active headlines.** "Capturing snowflakes up close with a camera." "Celebrating Danish heritage in western Iowa."
4. **Say "Iowa" with pride, not superlatives.** "Iowa's best burger" is a claim we back up in the story. Avoid empty hype words.
5. **Short sentences in UI; room to breathe in features.**
6. **Sentence case everywhere except the masthead, cover lines and kickers.**

### Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Cover lines | Punchy, concrete, 3 to 8 words | "Views of Iowa from above" |
| Story decks | Inviting, specific | "Plus the state of David Zollo, an Iowa farm dedicated to art, and Des Moines Playhouse as it begins its second century." |
| Subscribe | Clear value, no pressure | "Never miss an issue. 1 year for $24, 2 years for $38." |
| Gifts | Personal, warm | "Send a little bit of home to an Iowan you love." |
| Advertising | Confident, factual | "Reach readers who love Iowa." |
| Contributor calls | Welcoming, clear about expectations | "The Iowan is made of Iowa stories and Iowa photographs." |
| Social captions | Conversational, place-tagged | "Fog lifting off the Mississippi near McGregor this morning. Where's your favorite overlook?" |
| Errors | Calm, helpful | "That card didn't go through. Check the number or try another card." |
| Confirmations | Brief, grateful | "Thank you. Your first issue is on its way." |

### Vocabulary

| Prefer | Avoid | Why |
|--------|-------|-----|
| Iowans, readers | Users, consumers | We are a magazine, not a platform |
| Subscribe, renew, give a gift | Sign up for a plan, purchase a SKU | Plain language |
| Issue | Edition (except "digital edition") | Matches print usage |
| Town names, county names | "Rural area", "the heartland" | Specific beats vague |
| Small town | Hick, podunk, "middle of nowhere" | Respect for our readers' homes |
| Story | Content | We publish stories |
| Hidden gem (sparingly, once per issue at most) | "Must-see", "bucket list", "epic" | Overused travel clichés |
| — | Flyover country | Never, not even ironically |

### Prohibited Terms

| Avoid | Reason |
|-------|--------|
| Flyover country | Dismissive of our readers' home |
| Hick | Disrespectful to rural readers |
| Podunk | Disrespectful to small towns |
| Middle of nowhere | Every place we cover is somewhere |
| The heartland | Vague; name the place |
| Must-see | Travel cliché |
| Bucket list | Travel cliché |
| Epic | Hype |
| Content | We publish stories |
| Users | We have readers |

### Editorial style notes

- Follow AP style for news-style copy **[TO CONFIRM house style]**.
- "The Iowan" is italicized in running text when referring to the magazine in print contexts; on the web, plain text is fine.
- Write out county names in full: "Winneshiek County." On second reference, "the county."
- Regions: northeast Iowa, western Iowa, central Iowa (lowercase direction).
- Cities: "Des Moines," "Cedar Rapids," "the Quad Cities." No state after Iowa towns inside the magazine.
- Prices on the web: "$24" in running text, "$24.00" in carts and order summaries.

## 7. Photography and Imagery

Photography carries this brand. The covers prove the pattern: one strong, full-bleed Iowa photograph, real
light, real people, a sense of place.

### Photography style

- **Subjects:** Real Iowans in their own places; landscapes with scale (aerials, fields, rivers); interiors with character (brick, wood, neon, shelves).
- **Light:** Natural. Golden hour, blue hour, morning haze and night city light are all on-brand.
- **Color:** Rich and true, slightly warm. No heavy filters, no desaturated "moody" grading, no HDR halos.
- **Composition:** Leave clear sky or wall at the top third for the masthead, and a quieter edge for cover lines.
- **Seasons:** Show all four, including winter (snowflakes, frost, ice).
- **Authenticity:** No stock photography of Iowa. No AI-generated images presented as real places or people.

### Captions and credits

Every photograph is credited: "Photo by [Name]." Captions name the place: "Hot-air balloons over cornfields
near Indianola."

### Map and illustration

The "I am Iowan" tee uses the state outline filled with town names in type. That map-of-words motif is an
approved secondary graphic for merchandise, event signage and expat-focused campaigns. Don't use other
clip-art state outlines.

### Icons

Thin outline icons (1.5px stroke, 24px grid) in Prairie Ink or Iowan Teal, used sparingly in UI only. No icons in editorial pages.
