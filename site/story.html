A bookworm's guide to Iowa bookstores

[ONE-SENTENCE DECK FOR THE COVER STORY.]

By [AUTHOR NAME] · Photographs by [PHOTOGRAPHER] · [READING TIME] minute read

On a weekday morning the door opens before the lights are fully up, and the first customer already knows which shelf she wants. This is the rhythm of an Iowa bookstore: regulars who come for the conversation as much as the books, and travelers who wandered in from the square and stay for an hour.

The stores in this guide are small and independent. Most are run by a handful of people who read everything they stock. Each one reflects its town, whether that means a deep local history section, a children's corner with a rug worn thin, or a back room where a book club has met on the second Tuesday for years.
